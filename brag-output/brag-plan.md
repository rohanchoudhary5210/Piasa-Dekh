# Brag Plan: SalaryQuest (Piasa Dekh)

## What is this app?
SalaryQuest transforms monthly paycheck budgeting into a gamified RPG quest where discretionary funds become "Daily Mana", and daily underspending powers up a companion hero to defeat the monthly Boss.

## The angle
Personal finance apps are boring spreadsheets that guilt-trip you. SalaryQuest turns money discipline into a high-stakes RPG adventure with an obsidian luxury bento UI: overspending hurts your hero and borrows mana from tomorrow; saving up hits critical strikes on the end-of-month boss.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A stark obsidian slate ignites with violet lightning:
**"YOUR PAYCHECK IS NOT A LUMP SUM."**
Cut to a glowing Bento Mana Orb:
**"IT'S DAILY MANA: ₹1,450 / DAY"**

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Beat 1: The Daily Mana Gauge**: A sleek obsidian bento card shows today's budget counter ticking up to ₹1,450. A simulated coffee expense (₹220) deducts in real-time, flashing emerald "Safe Zone".
- **Beat 2: The Hero Companion & Streak**: Reveal Sir Reginald (Level 5 Knight, 85/100 HP, 14-Day Streak, 420 Coins). Overspending warning: "Borrowing mana damages hero HP".
- **Beat 3: The Monthly Boss Battle**: Confront the "End-of-Month Overspend Titan" with HP 10,000 / 10,000. Hero unleashes a "Surplus Vault Strike" dealt from disciplined daily savings!

## Outro / punchline
**"Level up your wallet. Defeat the Boss."**
Logo slam: **SalaryQuest (Piasa Dekh)**
Tagline: *Gamified Personal Finance in Flutter*

## User flow worth showing
Payday budget divided into Daily Mana → Log ₹220 Coffee (Mana gauge updates cleanly) → Hero gains +50 XP and 10 Gold Coins for staying in the Safe Zone → Vault strikes the monthly Boss.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Payday Awakening — 4.0s (0.0s – 4.0s)
Obsidian void. Massive high-contrast headline punches in with subtle glow: "YOUR SALARY IS NOT A LUMP SUM." Fast cut to the obsidian Bento Card revealing: "TODAY'S DAILY MANA: ₹1,450 / DAY".
Sequential/interaction: Counter ticks up from 0 to ₹1,450.
Audio intent: Atmospheric synth riser hitting a confident beat drop.
Audio-coupled idea: Counter tick SFX (`assets/sfx/interface/switch_001.ogg`) + impact bell (`assets/sfx/impact/impactBell_heavy_000.ogg`).
Transition mood: Hard cut to Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Dynamic Smoothing & Transaction — 4.5s (4.0s – 8.5s)
Inside the Bento Dashboard. Simulated transaction: "Blue Tokai Coffee ₹220". The mana ring calculates instantly. Status badge transitions to "SAFE ZONE • ₹1,230 REMAINING". Micro-caption: "Dynamic smoothing protects future days. Zero paise drift."
Sequential/interaction: Transaction chip slides in, Mana gauge settles, badge glows emerald.
Audio intent: Responsive tactile satisfaction.
Audio-coupled idea: UI tap click (`assets/sfx/interface/click_001.ogg`) on transaction log.
Transition mood: Seamless slide to Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — The Companion & The Boss — 5.5s (8.5s – 14.0s)
Split duel bento: On the left, Hero Companion "Sir Reginald" (Level 5 Knight • HP 100/100 • 14-Day Streak). On the right, "The Overspending Titan" (HP 10,000). The hero unleashes a "Surplus Vault Strike" dealing 1,230 DMG! Boss HP drops with damage floaters!
Sequential/interaction: Hero card slides in, sword strike animation across to Boss card, Boss flashes red.
Audio intent: Heroic RPG combat moment.
Audio-coupled idea: Metallic impact hit (`assets/sfx/impact/impactMetal_heavy_000.ogg`).
Transition mood: Dramatic scale-out to Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — The FinTech Masterpiece Outro — 4.0s (14.0s – 18.0s)
The entire obsidian mobile app frame materializes in center stage surrounded by glowing bento cards.
Headline: "SalaryQuest"
Subheadline: "Gamified Personal Finance & Daily Budget Engine"
Badges: "Local AES-256 Vault • Biometric Lock • Drift SQLite"
Audio intent: Music swell and smooth fade.
Audio-coupled idea: Final warm bell ring (`assets/sfx/interface/bong_001.ogg`).
Transition mood: End.
